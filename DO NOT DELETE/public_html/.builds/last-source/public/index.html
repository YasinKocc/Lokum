NOT JUST TEA.

Lokum is a community founded in October 2024 in Brussels with a simple idea: to create a warm and open space where people can come together, share stories, and feel at home, no matter where they come from.

Inspired by the spirit of Turkish hospitality, Lokum brings people together through cultural, social, and intellectual events. Our aim is not only to celebrate Turkish culture, but also to build bridges between cultures, encourage dialogue, and create meaningful connections across Belgium’s diverse society.

At the heart of Lokum are our regular Çay Akşamı evenings, moderated discussion gatherings where participants talk about everything from everyday life and personal experiences to philosophy, culture, and current issues, all over a cup of tea.

Alongside these, we organize special events such as cultural nights, interactive game evenings, and collaborative activities with other communities and associations.

Lokum is more than an organization. It is a space shaped by the people in it. We believe in inclusivity, respectful conversation, and the power of shared experiences to bring people closer together. Whether you join us to meet new people, explore different perspectives, or simply enjoy a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere, you are always welcome at Lokum.
